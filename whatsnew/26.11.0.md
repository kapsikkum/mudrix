## Calls, rebuilt

- **Calls are now built in**: faster to join, with screen sharing including audio, a volume slider for each person and fullscreen.
- **Watch YouTube together** right in a call (Beta).
- **Push-to-talk**, system-wide in the desktop app. Set it up in Settings -> Keybinds.

## Status and looks

- **Set a custom status** and see who's online, away or offline, if your server supports it.
- A new **compact message layout** and three new themes, **Pewter, Garnet and Blossom**, in Settings -> Appearance.

## Signing in

- **Sign in with a QR code** from another device that's already logged in.
- **Encryption sets itself up** in the background, so new sessions are ready right away.
- **matrix.to links** now open right in WizardChat.
