## New

### Spaces

- Auto-join is a setting of each space, and the space overview is tidier.
- A space sets its room list style for everyone, instead of each person choosing.
- Roles for Modern spaces: names, colours and groups for people in a space, set from a dropdown beside each member in the Members tab.
- Matrix style spaces manage rooms the Matrix way.
- Set a nickname per space from its menu, applied to your profile in every room of the space.

### Sounds

- New sound packs made for Mudrix: Bloop, Glass and Warm.
- Velvet is the default sound pack.

### Sign-in

- Sign-in checks your homeserver and says what it runs; autocorrect is off on the sign-in, sign-up and reset forms.
- A calmer, simpler sign-in screen.

### Profiles

- Show the game or music you're playing on your profile (desktop app, off by default).
- Glow design for the profile card, one surface with the banner fading into an accent wash.

### Messages

- Remind me on any message, with reminders listed and cancellable in Settings > Notifications.
- Delete on a focused message deletes it, and Shift skips the confirmation (also Shift-click in the menu).

### Activity

- Show a game and a song together next to the status, in a new Activity settings tab.
- Activity cards on the profile show the full game or track, and member rows put it ahead of the status.
- Member rows show an icon and the activity like Discord, and the profile cards use the full card width.
- The full profile's Activity tab leads with their game and track cards.
- Add your own applications to show as Playing, picked from the programs that are open.

### Other

- Join and leave sounds in voice channels.
- Sign in with a QR code, and link a new device from a verified session.
- Option to not share encryption keys with sessions that aren't verified.
- You appear in a voice room's participant list as soon as you join.
- A friendlier Home screen with a greeting, quick actions and setup steps.
- The update button turns into "Update to <version>" when a desktop update is found, and credits link WizardChat.
- Streamer mode blurs private details and hides notification text while you share your screen.
- Audit log in room and space settings shows who changed members, permissions, settings, rooms and roles.

## Fixed

### Spaces

- Auto-join waits until the space type is known and retries rooms that refused it.
- Auto-join is on by default in Modern spaces and retries after rate limits and network errors; roles no longer slow down busy accounts.

### General

- Verification requests already handled by another session are hidden.
- The DM list shows the default avatar when someone has no picture.
- The scroll-to-bottom button stays above the composer.
- Unread threads are worked out from read receipts, so they stay right across devices.
- Switching accounts restarts the app cleanly, so nothing carries over between accounts.
- QR sign-in keeps its encryption keys when you add a second account, and all-digit usernames can sign in.

### Other

- Sign-in tells an unreachable address apart from a site that isn't Matrix.
- Rooms with lots of undecryptable history no longer reload in a loop and say why messages can't be read.
- The status row spans the full card width in the full profile sheet.
- New rooms in a space default to Space members, Matrix-style spaces have no voice room type, and mutual rooms leave out categories and replaced rooms.
