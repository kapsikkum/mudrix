## Direct messages, one header

- **One header on phones**: search, inbox and the **+** that starts a chat. The wide "New message" pill and the old settings button are gone, and sorting lives in the **+** menu.
- **Nameplate rows** line up with the rest of the list; the presence dot stays on top and the preview stays readable over the art.

## A calmer timeline

- **Compact layout** reads like a log on iOS too: every line starts with its time and sender, with no extra line spacing.
- **Reactions** sit right under the message on Android and iOS, as they already did on the web.
- **Switching rooms** is smoother: Android crossfades and barely pans instead of pushing a whole screen, and iOS slides the rail and the chat on a spring.

## Fixes

- **iOS**: the compact layout can be picked again; bubble colours, the media viewer and the room layout are fixed.
- **Desktop**: attachments save through the system dialog, files drag in from Explorer, and notifications and read receipts follow the window again.
- **Cosmetics** packs load on every platform, and the call beta label is gone.
- **Links** in messages are underlined in the accent colour.
- The appearance previews call the demo user **Muddy**, like the rest of the app.
