## Messages, tidied

- **Compact layout** gives every line its own timestamp and sender again, instead of hiding them on the messages after the first in a run.
- **Bubble layout** keeps the sender name above a run and puts the time at the bottom of every bubble, so all bubbles line up.
- The same fixes are in the **Android and iOS** apps.

## One Mudrix on every screen

- **The web, desktop, Android and iOS apps now share one core**, so rooms, calls, settings and cosmetics behave the same wherever you sign in.
- **The desktop app is much smaller and lighter** — and from this release on it updates itself in place.
- **Android ships a signed APK**, also listed in the Mudrix app repo for F-Droid and SideStore.
- **iOS is an unsigned IPA** for SideStore or AltStore; no Apple account is needed to build it.

## Built for Mudrix

- **Galleries** keep a multi-picture send together: up to eight pictures in one tidy grid.
- **Markdown** works in messages: bold, italics, quotes, lists and links.
- **Your status dot stays on top** of nameplates, frames and decorations, so you can always see who is online.
- **Calls, the soundboard and profile cards** carry over with their Mudrix look.

## Fresh looks

- Two really dark themes, **Umbra and Eclipse**, join Carbon and the rest.
- Custom status, nameplates and avatar decorations are still there, and now follow you across devices.
