## Fixed

### Web

- Favourite GIFs in encrypted rooms again.
- Encrypted GIF favourites no longer store their keys in account data, and links, previews and embeds are more carefully contained.

### Other

- Media downloads are capped, call servers must be advertised by your homeserver, and sign-in callbacks are bound to the sign-in that started them.
- The desktop shell can no longer be navigated off the app, delete another folder's data, or write room keys outside a dialog-approved file.
- The iOS app keeps drafts and shared files protected, asks before joining a linked room, and hides chats in the app switcher.
- Android rejects unsafe attachment names, applies the network rules to link previews, and clears decrypted files on sign-out.
