## Find your way faster

- A new **title bar** puts search and your inbox at the top of the window.
- WizardChat now **remembers the last room** you had open in each space.
- **Drag rooms and spaces** to reorder them. The order syncs across your devices.
- New **keyboard shortcuts** for pinned messages, the inbox and moving between rooms and spaces. See them all in Settings -> Keybinds.

## Make it yours

- Adjust **text size, message spacing and UI density** in Settings -> Appearance.
- **Reduced motion** is now in Settings -> Accessibility.
- More of your settings now **sync to your account**, so new devices look the way you like.

## Chat and privacy

- Attachments **upload in the background** while you write, and are managed right in the composer.
- **Pinned messages** open in a quick popup.
- **Ignore users** and manage the list, or **block all invites**, in Settings -> Privacy.
- Create rooms and spaces with extra **founders** who share full control.
