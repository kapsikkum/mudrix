## New

- Archive a room without leaving it. It stays joined and syncing, moves into a collapsed Archived section, and comes back by itself when a newer message arrives. Archive and Unarchive sit above Leave Room and Leave DM on desktop, web and mobile.

## Fixed

- Direct-message notifications show the person's name once instead of repeating it as "person in person".
- GIF favourites no longer show broken images. Existing encryption keys are recovered from older saved data, while new keys stay only on the device that saved them.
- On iOS, the space rail slides over the chat instead of holding up room-opening and room-closing animations.
