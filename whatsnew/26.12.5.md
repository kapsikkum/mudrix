## New

- Links from X, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Pinterest, LinkedIn and Tumblr show as Discord-style cards with the author, text, pictures and video. The desktop and mobile apps load them through embeds.now once you allow a service; the browser keeps its simpler previews.
- Link embeds settings are redone: choose Ask, Allow or Hide for each service, or set them all at once.
- The soundboard has its own settings page, with your default pack and favourite packs, and it uses the standard format so clips play for people on other clients.
- Settings now run Emojis & Stickers, Cosmetics, then Soundboard, and Audio & Video is a single page.
- A Desktop settings page for starting with your computer, starting minimised, closing to the tray and muting notifications.
- A profile is one card, with no extra "View profile" step.

## Fixed

- Messages that could not be decrypted recover more reliably, including restoring keys from your backup.
- Opening Mudrix in a second browser tab warns you instead of breaking the first one.
- Refreshed sign-in tokens are saved safely on Windows.
- Read-receipt avatars and the side panels fit on narrow screens.
